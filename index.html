Privacy Policy for Storage Guardian

Application: Storage Guardian (com.aistudio.storageguardian.fzqpk)

Effective Date: October 6, 2026 | Version: 2.0

1. Introduction & Overview

Storage Guardian ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed to help users safely inspect, analyze, and free up Android device storage space, identify duplicate files, clear safe junk data, and optimize system responsiveness.

Your privacy is our utmost priority. Storage Guardian is built around a foundational principle: your private data stays on your device. We do not operate external storage databases for your personal files, nor do we upload, transmit, sell, or monetize your personal content.

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment: 100% of storage analysis, file scanning, duplicate byte hashing, image similarity ranking, and cleanable clutter detection is executed entirely on your device using local Android APIs. No photos, videos, audio, or documents are ever transferred to our servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional cloud-linked apps, Storage Guardian deliberately avoids collecting personal user data:

  • No Personal Files Collection: We never collect, copy, upload, or transmit your photos, videos, audio files, documents, or personal messages.
  • Strict WhatsApp & Messaging Data Protection: We never access, read, or delete your WhatsApp chats, WhatsApp media, WhatsApp databases, or backup files.
  • No Contact or Personal Identifiers: We do not access contacts, phone numbers, call records, SMS, email accounts, or account logins.
  • No Precise Location Tracking: We do not collect GPS coordinates or fine location data.

3. Strict File Safeguard Guarantee

To protect user data from accidental loss, Storage Guardian enforces hardcoded safeguard locks in its scanning and deletion engines:

🛡️ Never Deleted by Storage Guardian:

  • WhatsApp Data: Files located in WhatsApp directories (com.whatsapp, WhatsApp/Media, chats, documents, backups) are strictly whitelisted and locked against deletion.
  • Personal Camera Photos & Videos: Original primary photos and videos located in DCIM/Camera and personal galleries are safeguarded.
  • Personal Documents: User documents (PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, text files) are strictly protected.
  • Android System Files: Operating system partitions (/system, /data/system) are untouchable.
  • Mandatory User Confirmation: Every file removal requires transparent inspection and explicit confirmation by the user before permanent deletion.

4. Permissions Used & Purpose

Storage Guardian requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to perform device storage maintenance:

Permission Type Legitimate Purpose
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Runtime Identifies exact duplicates, screenshots, and image cache clutter locally on Android 13+.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO Runtime Detects oversized video files and screen recordings consuming excessive storage space.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Runtime Scans for user voice recordings and music files (excluding system ringtones).
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Install/Scoped Used on Android 12 and below (maxSdkVersion=32) to inspect downloads and cache files.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Allows optional alerts when available device storage falls below critical levels (under 15%).
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Required solely to retrieve and display advertisements via Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Storage Guardian is supported by advertisements served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous technical information to serve advertisements, such as:

  • Device information (manufacturer, device model, operating system version).
  • Pseudonymous advertising identifiers (in accordance with Android's Privacy Sandbox and Google Play policy).
  • IP address and general coarse location (country or city level).
  • Ad interaction and performance analytics.

Storage Guardian has configured the following privacy protections:

  • Ad personalization signals are turned off by default (google_analytics_default_allow_ad_personalization_signals = false).
  • Ad ID collection for analytics has been disabled (google_analytics_adid_collection_enabled = false).
  • AdServices Measurement API binding has been disabled (measurement allowAdServices = false) to protect user privacy.

For further details on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. Local Data Storage & History

Storage Guardian stores a local log of your cleanup history (date, bytes reclaimed, and category summary) on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database (Android Room). This information never leaves your phone and can be erased at any time by tapping "Clear History" in the Insights tab or clearing application storage in Android Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Storage Guardian does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third parties through our app, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Security

Because all file analysis and duplicate computations occur locally on your device, your files are protected by Android's built-in Linux kernel sandbox and Scoped Storage protections. We implement strict Android permission checks and never request root or unrestricted file system privileges.
